Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026 · Applies to the GReviewSumm Chrome extension, version 1.3.0.

The short version. There is no GReviewSumm server. Nothing is sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it. Review text goes only to the AI provider you choose — and if you choose a model running on your own computer, it never leaves your machine at all.

Who is responsible

GReviewSumm is built and maintained by an individual developer, minglau0315. Questions about this policy: minglau0315@gmail.com.

What the extension reads

Only when you click Analyze Reviews, and only from the tab you are on:

  • The text and star rating of the reviews shown on the page
  • The place name, its overall rating and review count
  • The category, address and phone number shown in the panel

It reads nothing until you ask it to. It does not run on other sites, does not read your browsing history, your other tabs, your bookmarks or your cookies.

Where that data goes

To exactly one place: the AI provider you selected in settings. Nowhere else. Which provider you pick decides whether anything leaves your computer.

Your choiceWhere review text goes
A local model — Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp, Jan, vLLM, KoboldCpp, LocalAI, text-generation-webui, or any OpenAI-compatible server you run To that server only. If it runs on your own machine or your own network, the review text never leaves it.
A hosted API — OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Groq, xAI To that company's API, over HTTPS, so it can generate the summary. Their own privacy policy then governs what they do with it.

If you use a hosted provider, their terms apply to the text sent: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, xAI. Choosing a local model avoids this entirely.

What is stored, and where

Everything is kept in Chrome's local extension storage on your own device. None of it is synced to us or to anyone else.

About API keys specifically

Keys are encrypted with AES-GCM-256 before being written to storage, using a key derived through PBKDF2-SHA-256 (100,000 iterations) from a per-install value and a random salt. Once saved, a key is never displayed back to you — the field shows a ✓ Saved badge instead. A key is only ever transmitted as the authorization header to the provider it belongs to.

An honest limit. This is meaningfully better than plain text, but it is not an OS keychain. Anyone with full access to your computer and to the extension's source could reconstruct the key. That is true of every browser extension that stores a credential; treat your device as the boundary.

What is never collected

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no usage statistics
  • No tracking, no advertising, no fingerprinting, no cookies
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no email collection
  • No crash or error reporting sent anywhere
  • No selling or sharing of data — there is no data of yours in our hands to sell

The extension's own interface makes no third-party requests at all. Its fonts are bundled rather than loaded from a font CDN, specifically so that opening the popup contacts nobody.

Your control

  • Remove a stored API key with the ✕ beside its field
  • Clear every cached summary and review set from History → Clear all
  • Reset to defaults in Advanced settings restores everything (your keys are deliberately kept, since they cannot be shown back to you)
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it — there is no copy anywhere else

Children

GReviewSumm is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the change is noted in the changelog. The extension's full source is available to read, so every claim here can be checked against the code.
